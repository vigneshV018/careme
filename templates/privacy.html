CareMe Privacy Policy & Safety Commitment

Emergency & Medical Disclaimer

Mandatory Regulatory Notice

CareMe is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or prescription modifications, and does not replace emergency services (112). CareMe is a remote observational and family communication platform designed to help aging parents and remote family circles coordinate daily schedules. In the event of an acute medical emergency, immediately contact licensed emergency services (dial 112 in India).

1. Elder Sovereignty & Explicit Consent

Elders maintain absolute control over their care circles. When any family member or caregiver registers with a Care Passcode, they enter in a locked pending status with zero access to health logs or location tracking until the elder explicitly taps Approve.

2. Granular Permissions Matrix

Elders can toggle 6 distinct permission boundaries at any time from their Care Circle screen (View health records, Edit medicines, Edit appointments, Receive alerts, View location, Manage access). Toggling off a permission takes effect immediately on the server side.

3. Zero Sale of Personal Data & Zero Behavioral Trackers

We never sell, monetize, or disclose your health data, medication schedules, or location coordinates to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or insurers. All data is strictly utilized to provide your family care features.

4. Complete Right to Deletion

You have the full right to delete your account, care circle, prescriptions, and historical logs at any time from your Settings page. Deletion is instantaneous and irrevocable across the database.
